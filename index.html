Before you go

A short traveler's guide

01

Pack light, pack right

One carry-on, two pairs of shoes, and a layer for every temperature you might meet. Anything more is weight you'll resent.

02

Learn ten words

Hello, thank you, please, and "where is" will open more doors than any app. Locals forgive the grammar and remember the effort.

03

Leave the plan loose

Book the first night and the last. Let the middle days be shaped by the people you meet and the weather you get.
